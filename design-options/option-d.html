Method
03

Method

Four steps, in order. The order is the point: a golden set before a prompt, a gate before a launch.

  1. 01

    Golden set first

    50 real cases from your traffic before any prompt work.

  2. 02

    Deterministic checks, then a judge

    Rules catch the cheap failures, a temperature-0 LLM judge grades the rest.

  3. 03

    A gate in CI

    The build fails on a quality drop, per intent.

  4. 04

    Cost per request, printed

    Every feature ships with its cost and latency on the dashboard.

The poster register continues the same 1.333 ratio three steps past t5 and is then set fluid: evidence numerals clamp to 13vw across two columns (26vw in one column on phones), the hero to 13.5vw. Only three things enter it: a number, the headline, and the monogram on the portrait plate.

Colour tokensOne dominant, one counter field, ink derived from the dominant hue. No second accent.

Spacing and radiusBase unit 8px · a bimodal ladder: tight inside a group, large between bands · radius 0, one value

s1 8 s2 16 s3 24 s4 40 s5 64 s6 104 s7 160

Radius: 0px on every surface, control and field. Borders: 1px hairlines for structure, 2px for controls and the table head rule. Elevation: none, because nothing here floats.
