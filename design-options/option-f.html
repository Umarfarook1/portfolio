Display

The hero sits above the six steps on a fluid clamp: clamp(46px, 9.4vw, 92px). At 1440px that is 92px, which is 5.4 times the 17px body. At 400px it is 46px, still 2.7 times body.

The three accents share lightness 0.780 and chroma 0.110 exactly, so they read as one family rotated through hue. Their tints share lightness 0.940. Nothing sits in the indigo to violet band.

Spacing and radius

Spacing unit 4px. Scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 104 · 144. Used bimodally: 8 to 16 inside a group, 72 to 144 between sections, nothing in between by accident.

Radius is rederived per role, never one reflex value: 6px focus ring, 10px inputs and thumbnails, 16px project and service cards, 999px pills.

Two hand-cut recipes. cut-a/b/c for evidence shapes, and the classic asymmetric hand recipe 255px 15px 225px 15px / 15px 225px 15px 255px for small notes and the contact box.

Motion

Hover and focus only. 180ms on cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .3, 1), transform and opacity and colour. Cards lift 3 to 4px and tilt one degree. Buttons lift 2px and press to 0.985. Nothing animates on load, nothing animates on scroll. Under prefers-reduced-motion the tilt token drops to 0 degrees and durations collapse to 1ms, so the states still read but nothing moves.
