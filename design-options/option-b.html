Chapter 09, The specimen

Design DNA

Adjectives: exacting, literate, plainspoken. Aesthetic: editorial magazine, style vector V7. Signature move: the marginalia column and its continuous spine. Refused: shadows, gradients, icon sets, a centred hero.
