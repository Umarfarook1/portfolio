The idea

one sentence

The site says she measures what she ships by printing a folio and marking, in pen, only the parts that were measured. Adjectives: composed, editorial, exact. Signature move: the pen only marks what was measured.

Nav and footer patterns

the rail, the contents, the colophon

Rail

Fixed 70px on the left from 768px up, a 56px bar at the top below it. It carries the burger, the edition mark, the brand, the year, and a one-pixel scroll bar. It takes its background, text and border from the chapter under its centre point and crossfades over 500ms.

Contents

A real dialog. Escape closes, main goes inert, focus lands on Close and returns to the burger. Items line-reveal, the current item keeps a pen rule under it.

Colophon

Near-black, a two word display heading revealed by words, the email as a permanently underlined link that clears to the right on hover, socials top right, the tagline bottom right.
