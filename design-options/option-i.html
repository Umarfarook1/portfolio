    What happens next

    • A reply within one working day, from a person.
    • A fifteen minute call, no deck.
    • A one page scope with a fixed price.

    Rates are launch pricing and hold until the first three clients are booked.
    Work is invoiced in two halves, on signature and on handover.
    The eval harness and the golden set are yours, in your repository, under your licence.
    Anything under a hundred megabytes of scanned data is inside the audit price.
    A regression found in the first thirty days after handover is fixed at no charge.
    Availability is two projects at a time, so the calendar is the real constraint.
    Time zone is Lisbon, with four guaranteed overlap hours for US teams.
    Reply by email is the default, and the toggle beside the form changes it.
